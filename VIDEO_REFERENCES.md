# SuriQ AI demo and pitch video references

SuriQ AI helps Philippine construction teams check supplier quotations against a bill of materials. Its strongest visual is a finding that opens the original BOM cell and quote line. The comparison matrix then shows why that difference matters to the buyer. [SuriQ workflow](README.md#review-a-batch)

## A possible 52-second Remotion cut

Use one fictional Philippine construction package and keep the same material, suppliers and units visible through every scene. This example assumes a short, caption-led product pitch.

| Time | On screen |
| --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:05 | Two document crops land side by side. **Cement · 120 bags required · 100 quoted.** Hold long enough to read both numbers. |
| 0:05–0:12 | Pull back to the batch. Drop in the BOM and two supplier quotations. Show the BOM and quotation roles appearing. |
| 0:12–0:22 | Open **Findings** and bring the quantity mismatch forward. Keep the item and both values visible. |
| 0:22–0:34 | Click **Focus highlights**. Match the position of the numbers in the finding to the numbers highlighted in the original Excel cell and PDF row. Pause on the evidence, then show the review decision being recorded. |
| 0:34–0:45 | Pull back to the supplier matrix and show the reason the incomplete offer is incomparable. End the workflow on the report export. |
| 0:45–0:52 | Close on SuriQ AI and **Verify supplier quotes against the BOM**. |

The product beats use the documented batch, findings, source view, comparison and export workflow. The quantities and suppliers are illustrative. [SuriQ workflow](README.md#review-a-batch)
